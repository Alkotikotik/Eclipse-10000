What GUCH needs from SM, reqs so i don't forget

//Offsets
offset in cycles of sm ports after operands arrival
0  : a, b, c, efp_op, int_op, int_sh, cexp_const
1  : neg_p, neg_c, cmp_signed, INMODE1
2  : INMODE2
3  : int_mul
4  : OPMODE2
5  : OPMODE3, ALUMODE3
11 : secret_code
14 : result, cmp_tru come out

OPMODE1 = 0110101 and ALUMODE = 0000 for every op.

//Float
FMA a*b + c     int_sh 0   cexp 28   INMODE 00000/00000   OPMODE2 1010101   OPMODE3 0011100   ALUMODE3 0000
    neg_c = 1 : a*b - c
    neg_p = 1 : -a*b + c
FCMP a cmp c same as FMA, b = 1.0 (0x40000000), neg_c = 1   //only cmp_tru counts

//Int ALU
ADD a + b: int_sh 25  cexp 28   INMODE 00000/00000   OPMODE2 0001100   OPMODE3 0010011   ALUMODE3 0000
SUB a - b: int_sh 25  cexp 28   INMODE 00000/00000   OPMODE2 0001100   OPMODE3 0010011   ALUMODE3 0011
AND a & b: int_sh 25  cexp 28   INMODE 00000/00000   OPMODE2 0001100   OPMODE3 0010011   ALUMODE3 1100
OR  a | b: int_sh 25  cexp 28   INMODE 00000/00000   OPMODE2 0001100   OPMODE3 0011011   ALUMODE3 1100
XOR a ^ b: int_sh 25  cexp 28   INMODE 00000/00000   OPMODE2 0001100   OPMODE3 0010011   ALUMODE3 0100
ICMP a cmp b: int_sh 24  cexp 28   INMODE 00000/00000   OPMODE2 0001100   OPMODE3 0010011   ALUMODE3 0011
                    cmp_signed = 1 : signed, 0 : unsigned   //only cmp_tru counts

//Compare codes
00 : ==
01 : !=
10 : <
11 : <=

//Mul
LOMUL   (a*b) >> k           int_sh 45-k  cexp 127    INMODE 00000/00000   OPMODE2 1010101   OPMODE3 0010101   ALUMODE3 0000   int_mul 1   //k 0..16
IFMA    (a*b + (c<<k)) >> k  int_sh 45-k  cexp 52-k   INMODE 00000/00000   OPMODE2 1010101   OPMODE3 0010101   ALUMODE3 0000   int_mul 1   //k 0..16
PREMUL  (a[23:0]*b) >> 24    int_sh 21    cexp 127    INMODE 00000/00000   OPMODE2 1010101   OPMODE3 0010000   ALUMODE3 0000   neg_p 1
HIMUL   (a[31:24]*b + c) >> 8  int_sh 37  cexp 52     INMODE 00110/00110   OPMODE2 1010101   OPMODE3 0010000   ALUMODE3 0000   neg_p 1
PREMUL and HIMUL are unsigned (neg_p = 1 means b unsigned), HIMUL's c is PREMUL's result.

//Shifts
SHL     a << k      int_sh 25+k  cexp 127   INMODE 00010/00010   OPMODE2 0001100   OPMODE3 0010000   ALUMODE3 0000   //k 0..31
SHR     c >> k      int_sh 45-k  cexp 52    INMODE 00010/00010   OPMODE2 1010101   OPMODE3 0010000   ALUMODE3 0000   //k 0..31
SRA     c >> k      int_sh 29-k  cexp 36    INMODE 00010/00010   OPMODE2 1010101   OPMODE3 0010000   ALUMODE3 0000   //k 0..29
SHR and SRA take the value from c, not a.
